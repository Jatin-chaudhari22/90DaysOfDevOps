---

##  My Understanding of DevOps & Cloud

DevOps is a culture and set of practices that combine software development and IT operations to improve collaboration, automation, and faster delivery of applications.

Cloud Engineering is about using platforms like AWS, Azure, or GCP to build, deploy, and manage scalable, secure, and cost-effective applications.

Together, DevOps and Cloud help organizations deliver reliable software quickly and efficiently.
